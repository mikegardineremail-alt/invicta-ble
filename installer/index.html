Invicta Bridge installer

Puts the battery-logger software on the Altronics Z6433 ESP32-S3 board. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

  1. Plug the board into the computer with a USB-C data cable, using the port labelled UART (or COM).
  2. Click the button below, pick the port that appears (CP2102 / "USB to UART"), then click Install.
  3. When it finishes it asks for your WiFi. Choose your home WiFi and type its password.
  4. Click Visit device to open the bridge's own page.
This browser can't do it. Please use Chrome or Edge on a Windows, Mac or Linux computer. This page must be opened over https.

No port listed? On Windows, install the CP210x driver, or try the other USB-C port. Still stuck? Hold the BOOT button, tap RST, let go of BOOT, and try again.
